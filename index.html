On the meeting of garment and light

Synode is the old word for a coming-together. Here it names the moment a garment meets the island light: linen that holds the afternoon, wool that remembers wind, silk that turns the colour of wet marble at dusk. Every plate in this volume was made outdoors, by available light, between the first and last hour of the sun.

We worked slowly. Each look was fitted on site and photographed in a single sitting, so that the folds you see were made by the walk to the frame and not by a stylist's pin. What follows is less a lookbook than a sequence: read it downward, as you would a manuscript.
